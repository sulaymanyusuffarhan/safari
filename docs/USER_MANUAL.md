# User manual — TBC-FTMS

## Roles covered
Employee (requester), Driver, Transport Officer, Department Head, Transport Supervisor.

## 1. Sign in
Go to the system URL, enter your username and password, and sign in. The default language is Kiswahili; switch to English using the top bar.

## 2. Request transport (Employee)
1. Click **Request transport**.
2. Select your directorate, department/unit and section.
3. Choose a transport category:
   - **News gathering / media coverage** — fill assignment title, coverage subject, media outlet, crew, urgency, additional destinations.
   - **Official institutional duties** — explain the official purpose.
   - **Regional / zone office transport**.
   - **Emergency transport** — flagged for priority assessment.
4. Enter departure location, destination, travel date/time, expected return, passengers, equipment/luggage and remarks.
5. Submit. You receive a reference number (e.g. `TBC-20261119-0001`) and can track the request under **My requests**.

## 3. My requests (Employee)
View status, assigned vehicle/driver and submit feedback after a journey. You may cancel requests subject to policy.

## 4. Driver dashboard (Driver)
View today's assignments and upcoming journeys. For each journey you can:
- Accept/acknowledge the assignment
- Confirm departure and record start odometer
- Record arrival and end odometer (distance calculated automatically)
- Record fuel, submit before/after-trip inspection checklist
- Report defects, breakdowns or accidents

Drivers **cannot** allocate vehicles to themselves or approve their own fuel.

## 5. Transport Officer dashboard
The TO sees the central fleet status board: total vehicles, available now, in use, assigned, under maintenance, out of service; pending requests; approved-but-unassigned requests; open breakdowns; maintenance due.

From here the TO:
- Reviews new requests and sees all suitable available vehicles (with reasons if none available)
- Allocates a vehicle and driver (system prevents double-booking and checks passenger capacity)
- Manages fuel allocation and consumption
- Coordinates maintenance and breakdown clearance
- Verifies electronic logbooks
- Generates weekly/monthly reports

## 6. Department head / supervisor
View and approve requests from your department; monitor transport utilisation and review completed trips. You cannot access unrelated departments' confidential records.

## Language
The interface defaults to Kiswahili. Use the language selector in the top bar to switch to English.
