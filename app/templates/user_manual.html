TBC Fleet and Transport Management System (TBC-FTMS)

How to use TBC-FTMS

  • Request transport: Employees submit a transport request via "Request transport", selecting category, route, date, passengers and purpose.
  • Approval: Department heads and transport supervisors review and approve/reject requests. A rejection reason is mandatory.
  • Allocation: The Transport Officer assigns a suitable available vehicle and driver. The system prevents double-booking and checks capacity.
  • Journey: Drivers start a journey, record odometer readings, and mark completion. Distance is calculated automatically.
  • Logbook: Every journey becomes an electronic logbook entry with full correction history.
  • Fuel: Record fuel transactions linked to vehicles, drivers and trips.
  • Maintenance: Log maintenance and repair records; track costs and status.
  • Breakdowns: Drivers report breakdowns; the vehicle is marked out of service until cleared.
  • Inspections: Record before/after-trip vehicle inspections.
  • Reports: View summaries and export transport request data as CSV.
  • Audit: Auditors view the immutable audit trail of who did what and when.
  • Language: Switch between Kiswahili and English using the top bar.
